# Asset register

Har image, illustration, animation, 3D model aur font jo site pe ship hota hai, uski ek row yahan honi chahiye. Admin mein upload hone wale media ke liye `license`, `credit` aur `sourceUrl` fields bharna zaroori hai.

**Rules**
- Sirf ye licenses: own/generated, public domain, CC0, CC BY (credit ke saath). Doubt ho to mat use karo.
- Kisi film, cartoon, brand ya news photo ka koi asset nahi.
- GIF nahi. WebP/AVIF (alpha), SVG, WebM, `.riv`, `.lottie`, Draco `.glb` (500 KB se kam).
- Thinker portraits: pehle Wikimedia Commons pe public-domain status verify karo, phir ek consistent style mein process karo (duotone ink + paper).
